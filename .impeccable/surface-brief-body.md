## Scope and mode

Complete navigable frontend prototype for the POLIS Observatory. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

Researchers monitor a live or completed AI-agent city, identify a macro change, trace it to events and agents, inspect an agent’s cognition and outcome, search the event log, compare runs, and review external-agent scorecards.

## Content and constraints

Use realistic synthetic fixtures labeled “Synthetic demo.” Preserve the eight specified views, read-only behavior, freshness indicators, persistent lag/sampling/drift states, inspector gaps, and comparison ordering. Backend integration is out of scope.

## Chosen direction

Living Systems Atlas with the approved Atlas Cockpit composition: an abstract city map owns the viewport, the atlas index anchors navigation, and a right evidence rail continuously links a macro anomaly to one selected agent’s perception, memory, decision, and outcome.

## Memorable moment

Selecting an agent on the city draws a cobalt evidence route across the map and opens the complete “why?” chain without leaving the city context.

## Unresolved decisions

None for the prototype. The approved comp’s satellite imagery and portrait treatment are not literal requirements; both become code-native abstract research graphics.
